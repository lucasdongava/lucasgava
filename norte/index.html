Important notice about fraud

Norte Group in Brazil does not offer loans, investment recommendations or courses through WhatsApp, phone, PIX, apps or social media.

This is the only official page of Norte Group in Brazil. Treat any communication using the Norte name outside official channels as suspicious and stop it immediately. The Group has identified fraudulent websites and WhatsApp groups promoting financial services and courses, and has reported them to the police.
